Tack Tack - Support

Tack Tack is a one-tap upwind sailing race. Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email russellbolger06@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

How to play

Tap once to start the 3, 2, 1 countdown. Tap after the gun to start sailing (tap before the gun and you get the black flag). After that, each tap tacks your boat. Reach the windward line first.

Common questions

Why was I disqualified? The game enforces the racing rules: port gives way to starboard (Rule 10), don't tack too close (Rule 13), and give room to tack at an obstruction (Rule 20). The result screen tells you which rule you broke.

Can I change the difficulty or number of boats? Yes. Tap Settings in the top right corner.

Does the game collect my data? No. See the privacy policy.
