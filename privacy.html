Tack Tack - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Tack Tack does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The game has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking, and it does not need an internet connection to play.

Your game settings (such as wind speed and difficulty) stay on your device. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
